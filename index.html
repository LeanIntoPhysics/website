How it works

Discrete first

Time steps, finite sums, lattices. The continuum arrives later, as the limit of a loop you have already written.

The math of a game engine

Vectors, matrix × vector, and v += a·dt; x += v·dt. Anything beyond that is introduced where a lesson needs it, and entered in a math ledger.

Every lesson proves something

The arguments are written in Lean 4 with Mathlib and checked by machine. Lean checks the mathematics; whether nature obeys the model is a separate question, and the lessons keep the two apart.

Built in public

Written with an AI tutor, and it says so. Each lesson ends with a record of what was corrected and who caught it.
